Cut video on your own machine.

LibreCut is a free, open-source, local-first video editor. Import media, edit a multi-track timeline, add effects, titles and music, then export an MP4 with FFmpeg. No account, no watermark, no telemetry.

What you can do

An early-stage editor built for fast, everyday cuts.

Multi-track timeline

Video, audio, text and effect tracks, a magnetic main track, ripple edits, snapping, split, trim and undo/redo.

Effects and transitions

Color adjustments, masks, blur, vignette, .cube LUTs, keyframes, speed ramps and every transition FFmpeg's xfade supports.

Audio tools

Ducking, EQ, panning, compression, per-track volume, automation curves and per-clip fades.

Titles and captions

Styled text with animation presets, plus .srt and .vtt import and .srt export.

Local AI

Captions, speech generation and image background removal on your machine. Models download on first use or install as offline bundles.

H.264 / AAC export

MP4 export with size presets, NVENC or CPU encoding, progress and cancellation. A cancelled export leaves no partial file.

Plugins and presets

Declarative effect-pack plugins and a community preset pack of color filters and text styles under CC0-1.0.

Templates and projects

Built-in and custom templates, autosave, and crash-safe .librecut project copies with a .bak backup.

Requirements and good to know

Taken from the project's own documentation.

Operating systemWindows 10/11 (verified). macOS Intel and Ubuntu 22.04 x64 are previews.
FFmpegBundled with the installers (9.0.1, essentials build). Nothing to install separately.
Disk spaceRoughly 4 GB for the app, FFmpeg, proxy cache and models.
Import formatsmp4, m4v, mov, webm, mkv, avi; mp3, wav, m4a, aac, flac, ogg; png, jpg, jpeg, webp, bmp, gif, avif.
ExportMP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio.
  • Only the first video and audio stream of each file is used.
  • Portrait rotation metadata is not applied to the picture; rotate the clip in the inspector.
  • The preview and the export are two renderers, so scaling, color and fonts can differ slightly.
  • If you use your own FFmpeg, keep it at 9.0.1 or older: 9.0.2 and later hang on timelines with a transition.

Read the full list of known limitations

Early-stage software. LibreCut 1.0.0 is dependable on Windows, but expect rough edges. The macOS and Linux packages exist and still need hands-on verification on supported systems.

Privacy and license

Local-first

  • Your media never leaves your computer.
  • No analytics, accounts or cloud dependency.
  • Update checks are off by default. If you turn them on, they contact this project's GitHub Releases only.
  • Local AI models contact Hugging Face only when a model is missing and Strict offline is off.
